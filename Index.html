The rhythm

Your four daily pings — 8:00, noon, 3:30, 7:30 — each mean one ~3-minute draw. Four draws lands you in your 10–15 minute window. The morning draw pairs with your guitar-and-music anchor: read one piece, then play.

What the formats are for

The ladder (five-year-old → expert) rebuilds one concept from first principles. Mentor studies distill the documented thinking of the people on your shelf. Contemplations are seeds for journaling — take the good ones into Claude and go deep. Micro-lessons end with a concrete action, where the $10k/month goal lives or dies on small daily moves.

How it's tuned to you

The generator knows your intellectual profile and your last 40 topics — it's instructed to never repeat itself, to skip the over-taught greatest hits, and to stay honest: established science and symbolic tradition are kept distinct, because you asked to be challenged, not validated.

The library is the point

Save what resonates. Over months this becomes a curriculum curated by your own attention. Prune it — removing is as much curation as saving.
